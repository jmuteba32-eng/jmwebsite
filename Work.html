Life Sciences Career

Over a Decade in Life Sciences

I've had the privilege of working across biotech and pharmaceutical organizations, contributing to the development of life-changing therapies and the systems that make them possible.

Across these engagements, I served in roles ranging from Quality Control Technician and Validation Engineer to Process Engineer, Project Manager, and Quality Assurance Manager — leading capital projects, driving compliance strategy, and executing GxP-regulated manufacturing operations.

Key highlights: traced 80% of deviations to six root causes at Regeneron, eliminating a 200–300 report backlog; designed a document intake system at Vertex driving 60 submissions across 20 owners; served as QA lead on ten facility-shutdown projects at Takeda.

Cambridge CIC — Early Stage Cohort No. 4

In Fall 2025, Brilla Life Sciences was selected for the Cambridge CIC Early Stage Cohort No. 4 — a prestigious program hosted at the Cambridge Innovation Center in Kendall Square. The program connects early-stage founders with mentors, investors, and a network of entrepreneurs. Through CIC, Jonathan gained strategic guidance and business development frameworks that directly sharpened Brilla's go-to-market approach and expanded its network in the Boston life sciences ecosystem.

ICCC Business Program

In December 2024, Jonathan completed the InnerCity Capital Connections (ICCC) Business Program — a nationally recognized accelerator that equips minority entrepreneurs with tools, strategies, and networks to scale their businesses and access capital. Through ICCC, Jonathan deepened his understanding of business development, financial strategy, and investor relations — applying those learnings directly to the growth of Brilla Life Sciences.
